# News analysis study pack

Use this original teaching pack with **Financial news to company & stock analysis**, immediately after Security Analysis in Quanta. The course contains 24 lessons, 12 hours of guided study, 48 practice questions, six checkpoints, and a final exam. Study time includes primary reading, calculations, and written assignments; exam time is additional.

All businesses, releases, forecasts, probabilities, and prices below are fictional. The packet is not a live news service or a recommendation.

1. Read `case-packet.md` without assuming later releases were available earlier.
2. Fill `research-memo.md`, separating facts, claims, and your assumptions.
3. Reproduce the capstone manually or run `python3 case_analysis.py` (Python 3.10+, standard library only).
4. Change the contract, working-capital, and required-return assumptions in the script. Explain the differences before reading its output.
5. Use the memo rubric to review the research reasoning separately from the automatic exam.

`glossary.md` connects English market terminology to Indonesian explanations. In Quanta, **Write in notebook** saves a lesson-linked assignment; use notebook export to retain your final memo.

The fictional documents are original Quanta teaching material. You may adapt this study pack for personal study and classroom exercises with attribution. Linked external publications retain their own rights; this permission does not cover them.
