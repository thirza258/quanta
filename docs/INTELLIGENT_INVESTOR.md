# The intelligent & careful investor

The course at `/curriculum/intelligent-investor` is directly before Security Analysis:
Trading Fundamentals → Intelligent & Careful Investor → Security Analysis → News
Analysis. Stable `11.1`–`11.20` lesson IDs are independent of displayed track order.
Algorithm Evaluation remains the final track.

Twenty original lessons in five units budget ten hours of guided study. Themes
include investor temperament, investment versus speculation, defensive/enterprising
approaches, market fluctuations, goals and liquidity, inflation, diversification,
margin of safety, allocation, rebalancing, fees, contribution discipline, disclosure
reading, adviser incentives, fraud checks and decision journals.

Each lesson has a worked case, equation, executable arithmetic worksheet, two
practice questions, assignment, solution and references. Four four-question
checkpoints require 3/4; the 20-question version-2 final requires 16/20. Exam time
is additional to the study budget. A separate investor-policy capstone has a
100-point self-assessed rubric.

The [downloadable workbook](../content/projects/intelligent-investor/README.md)
contains a thematic reading guide, investor-plan template and original worked-case
checks. It is inspired by Benjamin Graham's *The Intelligent Investor*, with book
information attributed to Jason Zweig and modern education/disclosure references
from Investor.gov and the SEC. It does not reproduce the book, chapter text or
publisher exercises. Modern planning, fund and fraud topics are labeled as course
extensions. Learners use their own copy for original reading; edition pagination
is not assumed.

Case weights, thresholds and rupiah amounts are fictional. The course builds a
reasoning process rather than certifying personal suitability. Worksheets calculate
the cases and keep the price sandbox in cash. The next Security Analysis course
develops the deeper company, statement and security-valuation questions.
