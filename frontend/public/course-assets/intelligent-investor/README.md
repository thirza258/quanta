# The intelligent & careful investor

An original Quanta companion inspired by Benjamin Graham's *The Intelligent
Investor*, with modern source-checking and planning exercises. It follows Trading
Fundamentals and precedes Security Analysis. The 16 lessons budget eight hours of
study, plus four unit checkpoints, a final concept exam and optional further reading.

Read the named themes in your own legally obtained copy. Editions and pagination
vary. This download includes original exercises and a plan template, not the book,
publisher exercises or reproduced chapter text. There is no affiliation with the
authors or publisher. A reference is an attribution, not an endorsement.

## Reading map

| Unit | Book themes | Original course applications |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Think carefully | Investment/speculation, defensive/enterprising approaches, market fluctuations | Decision quality, workload budget and behavioral response |
| 2. Protect the plan | Inflation, diversification and margin of safety | Liquidity pools, purchasing power, overlapping funds and downside cases |
| 3. Maintain a portfolio | Portfolio policy and investment funds | Scenario allocation, drift, fee arithmetic and contribution schedules |
| 4. Exercise judgment | Security analysis, advisers and independent judgment | Earnings reconciliation, verification, decision journal and investor policy |

Modern fund structures, fraud checks and household planning examples are extensions;
they are not presented as Graham's historical product or allocation recommendations.
Case amounts, weights and thresholds are fictional, not personal prescriptions.

## Workbook

Complete `investor-plan.md` alongside the lessons. Use `worked-cases.md` to check
arithmetic after attempting it. All lesson worksheets run in Quanta's Python
sandbox without coding prerequisites; they compute the case and keep the price
simulation in cash. They do not implement or validate a Graham trading strategy.

The written plan is self-assessed separately from the server-graded concept exam.
Finish by listing the disclosure questions you will investigate in Security Analysis.
