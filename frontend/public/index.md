# Quanta — Investment learning, in practice

Quanta is an interactive workspace and comprehensive platform for fundamental valuation, quantitative finance, econometric modeling, and reproducible investment research.

## Core Curriculum & Modules

Quanta provides twelve extensive courses spanning market mechanics, security analysis, econometric tools, algorithmic trading, and modern portfolio theory:

1. **Trading & Market Fundamentals**: Order books, market microstructure, liquidity, execution, and transaction cost analysis.
2. **The Intelligent & Careful Investor**: Value investing philosophy, margin of safety, portfolio allocation, risk management, and investor psychology.
3. **Security Analysis**: Fixed income valuation, balance sheet analysis, financial statement forensics, equity valuation, and committee capstone.
4. **Financial News to Company & Stock Analysis**: Macro releases, central bank policies, commodity shocks, and corporate earnings analysis.
5. **Quantitative Investment Analysis**: Time series econometrics, statistical inference, factor models, and quantitative backtesting.
6. **The Strategy Lab**: Quantitative alpha factors (value/quality, momentum/reversal, pairs trading, ML signals), risk parity, and portfolio construction.
7. **Python for Quantitative Finance**: Vectorized backtesting, NumPy/Pandas pipelines, and risk attribution.
8. **High-Performance C Trading Systems**: Low-latency order matching, memory-efficient data structures, and HPC simulations.
9. **Machine Learning in Asset Pricing**: Supervised learning for cross-sectional return prediction, feature engineering, and shrinkage methods.
10. **Alternative Data & Web3 Finance**: On-chain data analysis, smart contract liquidity, and decentralized exchange microstructure.
11. **Risk Management & Portfolio Stress Testing**: Value at Risk (VaR), Expected Shortfall, stress tests, and drawdown controls.
12. **Algorithm Evaluation & Institutional Reporting**: Performance tear-sheets, Sharpe/Sortino/Calmar ratios, and reproducible research reports.

## Features

- **Hands-on Labs & Sandboxes**: Executable notebooks, interactive code runners, and dataset explorers.
- **Reproducible Research**: Pre-loaded market data, Parquet storage, and deterministic backtesting harnesses.
- **Progress Tracking & Assessments**: Chapter checkpoints, comprehensive problem sets, and practical capstone reviews.
- **Self-Contained & Privacy-Focused**: Run locally or in private clouds with PostgreSQL and Redis.
